About Wheat Diseases

Wheat is susceptible to various diseases that can significantly reduce yield and quality. Early detection and proper management are crucial for maintaining healthy crops.

Common Wheat Diseases:
  • Leaf Rust: Fungal disease causing orange-brown pustules on leaves
  • Yellow Rust: Stripe rust disease with yellow-orange stripes
  • Mildew: Powdery mildew affecting leaves and stems
  • Wheat Loose Smut: Fungal disease replacing grains with black spores
